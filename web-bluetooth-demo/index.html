GBV Safety Watch

Web Bluetooth demo · live heart rate from your MOYOUNG / Da Fit watch
This browser does not support Web Bluetooth. Use Chrome or Edge on Windows/Android. (Safari and iPhone are not supported.)
Before you start: close/unpair the official Da Fit app, keep the watch on your wrist with heart-rate monitoring enabled. This demo reads the standard Heart Rate service only and cannot send SMS or run in the background — it is a connection proof, not the real app.
Ready. Click "Connect watch" and pick your device.
After connecting, this lists every GATT service/characteristic the watch exposes and sends the MOYOUNG GPS request command, dumping any raw response bytes to the log below. Go outside or near a window for a GPS fix.
Subscribes to EVERY notify/indicate characteristic the watch exposes, then logs any raw bytes that arrive. After clicking, press the watch's physical buttons repeatedly and watch the log. If bytes appear on a button press, that characteristic is a usable button channel. If nothing appears across all buttons, this watch does not report button presses over Bluetooth.
