🛡️ GBV Safety Watch

Open-source proof of concept — a smartwatch + app that detects distress and alerts emergency contacts. These are the browser demos.
❤️ Heart Rate & Watch Probe →
Connect a BLE smartwatch, stream live heart rate, and probe its Bluetooth services. (Chrome/Edge only — not iPhone.)
🚨 SMS Alert Test & Panic Trigger →
Build the real alert message with live GPS, trigger via triple-tap, and send a real SMS through your phone. (Works on iPhone.)
📡 Alert Receiver Screen →
A contact / responder dashboard that lights up when an alert is triggered (same browser).
These are demos, not the finished product. Real automatic SMS, background monitoring, and on-device GPS live in the native app — see the GitHub repo.
MIT licensed. Built as a non-commercial POC for the safety of people at risk of Gender-Based Violence. Contributions welcome.
